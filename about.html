About Colorless

Colorless is a free reference workspace for artists, illustrators, and creatives who want to understand a reference before drawing or painting.

Identify colors, extract palettes, study values, simplify references, and build precise drawing grids — all in one focused workspace.

What the tools are for

🎨 Color ID

Pick a point in your reference to study its exact color.

▦ Grid

Use a grid to transfer proportions and placement accurately from your reference to your canvas.

✂️ Crop

Frame your reference to match your canvas or focus attention on the composition.

✨ Palette

Extract key colors from your reference and study their relationships before you start mixing paint.

◐ Value

Reduce color so you can concentrate on light, midtones, and shadows.

◒ Simplify

Reduce visual detail into larger shapes so the main forms and values are easier to understand.

🎨 Color Mixer

Experiment with colors before committing them to your artwork.
